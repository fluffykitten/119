# Design Direction: Element 119+ (IUPAC Student Synthesis Dossier)

## Product Identity
* **Application**: Element 119+ (IUPAC Student Synthesis Dossier)
* **Creator Attribution**: Designed and Created by Mr. Salman (fluffykitten)
* **Audience**: High school chemistry students and teachers (IGCSE & AS-Level Chemistry curriculum)
* **Visual Aesthetic**: Strict Paper Archival Document (matte ink-on-paper, precision laboratory report)

## Dial Settings
* **ENERGY: 2** (Balanced laboratory precision with purposeful collision feedback)
* **RHYTHM: 2** (Sequential 4-step wizard with deliberate structural variation)
* **MOTION: 2** (Canvas-driven cyclotron beamline physics and shader transformations)

## Color Palette (Max 3 Cores + 1 Accent)
* **Core Base Neutral**: `slate-50` (`#f8fafc`) and `slate-100` (`#f1f5f9`) paper background
* **Core High-Contrast Ink**: `slate-900` (`#0f172a`) and `slate-800` (`#1e293b`) typography
* **Core Structural Rules**: `slate-300` (`#cbd5e1`) hairline 1px borders and millimeter ticks
* **Deliberate Scientific Accents**:
  * `cyan-700` (`#0e7490`): Cherenkov telemetry & beamline focus
  * `rose-700` (`#be123c`): IUPAC Verification official stamp

## Typography System
* **Primary Headings & Element Symbols**: `Space Grotesk` / `Inter` (tight tracking, high legibility)
* **Technical Telemetry & Configurations**: `JetBrains Mono` (tabular numbers, subshells, mass specs)

## Visual Motifs & Precision Accents
* Registration crosshairs (`+`) at card corners
* Millimeter calibration tick marks along document margins
* NFPA 704 standard 4-quadrant hazard diamond (Health, Flammability, Instability, Special)
* Quantum orbital electron ring overlays
* Authentic Code-128 styled barcode serial numbers
* Official matte red/slate "IUPAC SYNTHESIS VERIFIED" laboratory stamp
* Minimalist grayscale vector tabby cat badge honoring Mr. Salman (fluffykitten)

## Antislop Compliance
* Strictly zero em dashes (`—`) across all UI copy, flavor texts, and chemistry notes
* No generic marketing buzzwords; strictly authentic IGCSE/AS-level scientific terminology and curriculum humor
* Minimum 44px tap targets for mobile/touch screens
* Full keyboard navigability (Tab focus rings, Enter triggers, Escape for modals)
* Robust fallback states: Loading cyclotron, empty classroom shelf, and camera permission error handling
